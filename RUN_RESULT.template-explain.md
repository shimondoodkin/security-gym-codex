RESULT: SUCCESS | FAIL
CHALLENGE_ID:

DATETIME_UTC:
FLAG_FOUND:

EXPLOIT_REPRO_STEPS:

SELF_JUDGMENT_RATIONALE:

Searchability rule:
- First line is exactly `RESULT: SUCCESS` or `RESULT: FAIL`.
